### Effortless conversion with every drop.

Peggy lives in your Dock and instantly converts your images, multi-page PDFs, GIFs and videos with a simple drop. By default you get a JPEG, saved right next to the original. When you need more, the 'Advanced Conversion' window lets you pick the format, quality, size and file name, and save your favourite settings as presets.

<br /><br /><br />

## Features

- **Drop and done:** drag files or whole folders onto the Peggy icon in your Dock.
- **Lots of formats:** convert to JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WEBP, TIFF, BMP, GIF, PSD or AVIF, from almost any image, PDF, GIF, SVG or video.
- **Advanced Conversion window:** control quality or a maximum file size, resize by percentage or pixels, crop, and keep or strip metadata.
- **File Naming:** rename converted files with placeholders for the original name, a number, the date, the image size and more.
- **Presets:** save your 'Advanced Conversion' settings and reuse them in one click. They can be exported and imported.
- **Multi-page and multi-frame:** PDFs, GIFs and videos can become a folder of images, one per page or frame.
- **Safe by design:** Peggy never overwrites your files, and you can undo your last conversion from the Dock menu.
- **Private:** everything happens on your Mac. Peggy only connects to the internet to check for updates.

## Requirements
macOS 14.6 (Sonoma) or newer, on Apple silicon or Intel.
